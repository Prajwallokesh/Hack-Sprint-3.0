03 / THE REGULATIONS

RULEBOOK FOR
HACKSPRINT 3.0

Official competition rules and guidelines established by COMPASS × GLUE for all participating teams at Government Engineering College, Hassan.

  • 01

    GEC Hassan teams: Maximum 3 members per team (2 first-year students will be added by the organizers).

  • 02

    COMPASS association preference: Students who have paid the COMPASS association fee will get first preference during team selection and slot allotment.

  • 03

    Other college teams: 3 to 5 members per team with valid physical college ID.

  • 04

    Fresh code only: All code must be written from scratch during the hackathon—no pre-built code allowed.

  • 05

    Problem statements: Official problem statements will be revealed live on spot at the venue.

  • 06

    24-hour offline build: All participants must stay on campus at GEC Hassan for the full 24 hours.

  • 07

    Mentor reviews: Every team must attend 3 progress review checkpoints with mentors during the sprint.

  • 08

    Working demo: Teams must present a working project demo to the judges—slides only are not accepted.

  • 09

    Fair play: Any copying, cheating, or misconduct will lead to immediate disqualification.

05 / EVENT TIMELINE

EVENT SCHEDULE.
24-HOUR TIMELINE.

All events and activities take place at the Library Block from 2:00 PM on Nov 14 to 2:00 PM on Nov 15.

VENUE: LIBRARY BLOCK
  1. 02:00 PM – 03:00 PM NOV 14 • DAY 1
    REGISTRATION LIBRARY BLOCK

    Team Registration & Desk Setup

    Teams arrive at the venue, complete check-in, and set up their laptops.

  2. 03:00 PM – 03:30 PM NOV 14 • DAY 1
    INAUGURATION LIBRARY BLOCK

    Opening & Welcome Ceremony

    Welcome speech, introduction of mentors, and event guidelines.

  3. 24-HOUR HACKATHON BEGINS
    03:30 PM SHARP NOV 14 • DAY 1
    HACKATHON START LIBRARY BLOCK

    Problem Statements Launch & Hacking Starts

    Problem statements are revealed and the 24-hour coding sprint officially begins.

  4. 05:00 PM – 05:30 PM NOV 14 • DAY 1
    TEA BREAK LIBRARY BLOCK

    Tea & Snacks Break

    Hot tea and snacks served with initial mentor discussions.

  5. 08:30 PM – 09:30 PM NOV 14 • DAY 1
    DINNER LIBRARY BLOCK

    Dinner Break

    Dinner break for all participants and team progress check.

  6. 10:00 PM – 11:30 PM NOV 14 • DAY 1
    MENTORING LIBRARY BLOCK

    Mentoring Round 1

    Mentors visit teams to check ideas, architecture, and project setup.

  7. 12:00 AM MIDNIGHT NOV 15 • DAY 2
    MIDNIGHT SNACK LIBRARY BLOCK

    Midnight Snacks & Short Break

    Hot Maggi, tea, coffee, and short fun activities to recharge energy.

  8. 03:30 AM – 04:30 AM NOV 15 • DAY 2
    CODING SPRINT LIBRARY BLOCK

    Night Coding Sprint

    Teams build core project features with hot coffee available all night.

  9. 07:30 AM – 08:30 AM NOV 15 • DAY 2
    BREAKFAST LIBRARY BLOCK

    Morning Breakfast

    Fresh morning breakfast and coffee to start the final build hours.

  10. 10:00 AM – 11:30 AM NOV 15 • DAY 2
    MENTORING LIBRARY BLOCK

    Mentoring Round 2

    Mentors review working prototypes and give final demo tips.

  11. 12:30 PM – 01:00 PM NOV 15 • DAY 2
    CODE FREEZE LIBRARY BLOCK
